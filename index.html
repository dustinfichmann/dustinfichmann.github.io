A short introduction

Before Berkeley, I was a Predoctoral Research Fellow with Matthew Rabin at Harvard. Before that, I worked for Thomas Graeber at Harvard Business School.

I completed an M.A. in Economics at Columbia and a B.A. in Mathematics and Economics at Bates (summa cum laude, Phi Beta Kappa). At Bates, I was a Davis UWC Scholar and a Dana Scholar. I also studied as a visiting student at Oxford. Before university, I attended UWC Costa Rica.
